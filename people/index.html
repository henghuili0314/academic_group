Xin Feng

Research: Computer Vision, Image & Video Processing
冯欣
博士 · 硕士生导师
研究方向：计算机视觉、图像及视频处理
I am a Ph.D. holder and MSc supervisor, recognized as a key academic talent in Chongqing. Before that, I completed my postdoctoral research at the NYU Tandon School of Engineering. My work centers on intelligent visual perception for unmanned systems, adversarial attacks on neural networks, and medical image processing. Over the years, I have published more than 40 papers in top venues such as TPAMI, CVPR, NeurIPS, and ICCV, and hold 14 patents. I have led 16+ provincial and ministerial-level projects, including NSFC grants, with total funding exceeding 3 million RMB. Several of my results have been put into production at Changan Auto and Shanghai Huayu Vision, generating economic benefits of over 10 million RMB. I was also awarded the "Young Elite" and "Lingjun Talent" titles by Chongqing University of Technology.
冯欣，博士，硕士生导师，重庆市学术技术带头人后备人选；美国纽约大学工程学院电子工程系博士后。 致力于无人系统智能视觉感知、神经网络模型对抗攻击、医学图像处理及辅助诊疗等研究。 在 TPAMI、CVPR、NeurIPS、ICCV 等顶级期刊/会议上发表论文 40 余篇，授权专利 14 件； 主持国家自然科学基金青年基金、重庆市自然科学基金等多项省部级项目 16 项，承担经费 300 余万元。 相关成果已在长安汽车、上海华域视觉科技等企业完成产业化应用，产生经济效益 1000 万元以上。 获重庆理工大学首批"青年英才"及"士继英才·领军人才"称号。
